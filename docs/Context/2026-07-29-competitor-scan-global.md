# Competitor Scan — Global Incumbents & Member-App Feature Bar

**Date:** 2026-07-29
**Scope:** English-speaking global gym-management incumbents and the member-facing app feature bar they've set. Mexico/LatAm-local vendors are explicitly out of scope (owned by a parallel research lane).
**Method:** Web search across vendor pricing pages, Capterra/G2/GetApp/Trustpilot aggregators, and community forums (Reddit, vendor community boards). Vendor primary pricing pages are frequently gated behind "request a demo"; where so, this report uses third-party/operator-reported numbers and flags them as lower-confidence.

---

## Verdict (one paragraph)

The global market is a two-tier stack: a handful of legacy enterprise platforms (Mindbody, ABC Fitness's family — Ignite/Glofox/Trainerize/Evo — Zen Planner, WellnessLiving, Mariana Tek) that have drifted to $150–$700+/month per location *before* add-ons, plus a payment-processing rake of roughly 2.75–3.6% + $0.30 per transaction that is mandatory or heavily steered, and a wave of newer/leaner challengers (Gymdesk, TeamUp, PushPress, Xplor Triib, Gymflow, Clubworx, Fitli, Momence) pricing flat-rate SaaS from $75–$250/month explicitly to court the same 1–3 location independent gyms this product targets, several with *no* forced payment-processor cut (Gymflow, TeamUp, Zen Planner, Gymdesk are non-freemium/no-take-rate models; PushPress and Momence use a free/low-tier-plus-processing-cut model that inverts to a take rate at the bottom). "We take no cut of member payments" is therefore a real differentiator against the legacy tier (Mindbody/Glofox/WellnessLiving/Momence-free/Arketa all layer a transaction-based cut on top of subscription) but not a novel one against the direct-competitor challenger tier, where flat, no-rake pricing is already normal — the wedge is real but must be paired with the *branded member app being standard, not a $97–$349/month or one-time-fee upsell*, since that add-on tax is where almost every incumbent — including several challengers — still extracts money.

## Per-vendor detail (expanded notes beyond the table)

**Mindbody** — The category's most cited incumbent and the most-complained-about on price escalation. Base subscriptions of $139–599/mo commonly land near $1,000+/mo in real spend once marketplace commission, processing markup, and add-ons are counted ([FitBudd](https://www.fitbudd.com/post/how-much-does-mindbody-cost), [Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/mindbody-pricing/)). Contracts run 90-day non-cancelable + 12-month auto-renew with 30–50% early-termination penalties, plus a reported ~$500 data-export fee disclosed only at cancellation ([Vibefam Reddit synthesis](https://vibefam.com/mindbody-reviews-reddit-2026/)).

**ABC Glofox** — Owned by ABC Fitness (a Thoma Bravo portfolio company that also owns Ignite, Trainerize, and Evo — [abcfitness.com](https://abcfitness.com/)). Pricing is entirely quote-gated beyond "starting at $99/mo" ([Glofox plans page](https://www.glofox.com/plans/)); the single most-repeated 2024–2025 complaint is a **70% mid-contract price increase** reported by operators ([Vibefam](https://vibefam.com/glofox-pricing-2026/)). Capterra aggregate is 4.4/5 (354 reviews) but customer-service response time and recurring booking-flow bugs are the top 1-star themes ([Vibefam Capterra synthesis](https://vibefam.com/what-glofox-users-actually-say-on-capterra-2026/)).

**ABC Ignite / ABC Fitness** — The largest player by raw volume: 26–35M members, 24,000–30,000+ clubs, $8.8B+/yr processed across 90 countries ([abcfitness.com](https://abcfitness.com/), [ignite.abcfitness.com](https://ignite.abcfitness.com/)). This is the "big box"/franchise end of the market, not a direct competitor for a 50–500-member independent gym, but it defines what "enterprise" gym software looks like and its scale confirms payment processing is core to ABC's business, not incidental.

**ABC Trainerize** — Notable for having the market's clearest *two-tier* branded-app pricing precedent: a cheap **$169 one-time** re-skin at the Pro tier vs. a **fully included** true white-label App Store listing at the $248–275/mo Studio tier ([trainerize.com/pricing](https://www.trainerize.com/pricing/)). This is a good comparable for how to price (or not price) a branded app tier.

**Wodify** — Best-liked of the CrossFit-native tools in review aggregate (Trustpilot 4.4/31, ~81% 5-star; G2 166 five-star of 195) ([search synthesis, Capterra/Trustpilot/G2 pages]). Genuinely competitive processing rates (as low as 2.6%+$0.25 card, 1.5%+$0.30 ACH, no monthly processing fee) ([wodify.com/pricing](https://www.wodify.com/pricing)).

**PushPress** — The clearest "freemium funded by processing rake" model in the set: **Free tier costs nothing in subscription but charges 4.99%+$0.30 per transaction**, undercut to 2.75–2.89% as you pay $159–229/mo ([PushPress blog](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/what-does-gym-software-really-cost)). Most-recommended pick for new CrossFit/boutique gyms in community discussion, but draws real reliability complaints ("constantly down or breaking when they release new features") and billing/re-charge bugs ([Vibefam Reddit synthesis](https://vibefam.com/gym-management-software-reddit-2026/)).

**Gymdesk (Gym Insight)** — Explicitly positions on price transparency against Mindbody-tier incumbents; member-count-tiered flat pricing from $75–$200/mo with no forced processor ([gymdesk.com/blog/gym-management-software-cost](https://gymdesk.com/blog/gym-management-software-cost)). Note the *legacy* Gym Insight brand instead charges **$499/mo** if a gym insists on bringing its own merchant account/gateway — i.e., BYO-processor is priced as a premium, the inverse of this product's stance ([gyminsight.com/pricing](https://www.gyminsight.com/pricing)).

**TeamUp** — UK/EU-weighted, scales price by active-customer count rather than feature tier, $104–309/mo, no setup fee ([teamup.com/pricing](https://www.teamup.com/pricing/)).

**Virtuagym** — Genuinely has a native white-label app (own App Store/Play listing) but buries both general pricing and the app's specific cost behind a sales call — the least pricing-transparent vendor surveyed after Glofox/Hapana ([business.virtuagym.com/our-prices](https://business.virtuagym.com/our-prices/)).

**Clubworx / Fitli / Gymflow / Everfit / Arketa** — The "challenger" cluster; largely flat, published, transparent pricing aimed at owner-operators, several explicitly marketing "no lock-in" (Gymflow: 30-day notice cancellation, [gymflow.io/pricing/usa](https://www.gymflow.io/pricing/usa)). Arketa is the outlier here with a **3% platform transaction fee stacked on top of card processing** ([Vibefam](https://vibefam.com/arketa-pricing-2026/)) — worth noting since it undercuts its own "modern/affordable" positioning.

**WellnessLiving** — Mandatory in-house **Paragon** payment processor; exact rate isn't published but the vendor's own community forum has threads titled "Paragon ridiculous fees" describing them as "borderline predatory" ([community.wellnessliving.com](https://community.wellnessliving.com/topic/paragon-ridiculous-fees/)). The white-label **Achieve** app is gated to the $349/mo+ BusinessPro tier — a hard, explicit wedge point against a product that ships branding by default.

**Momence** — The most transparent "your subscription price buys down the take rate" model in the market: Free = 5% of every transaction, $60/mo Pro = 2.5%, $199/mo Custom = 0% ([Studiostackpro/Vibefam pricing breakdowns](https://studiostackpro.com/blog/momence-pricing-guide/)). For a $100k/yr-revenue studio this nets out to roughly $12.9k/yr (Basic) vs. $3.9k/yr (Pro) vs. $6.3k/yr (Custom) in total platform+processing cost — a useful real-world anchor for how expensive "free software" actually is once volume is high.

**Hapana** — Modular, high-end, quote-gated; the white-label app is reserved for the top module bundle (from $635/mo) ([hapana.com/pricing](https://www.hapana.com/pricing)) — another explicit "branding costs extra, a lot extra" data point.

**Mariana Tek / Xplor Triib / Perfect Gym / Twin Oaks / SportsNow** — All thinner on public pricing; treat as enterprise/regional-niche players rather than direct competitive pricing anchors. Xplor (parent of both Mariana Tek and Triib) rolls up several of these acquired brands the same way ABC Fitness does — consolidation into a small number of PE-owned holding companies is the dominant industry structure at the top of the market.

## Table-stakes feature bar (what "everyone in this category has")

Based on the vendors surveyed, the following are functionally universal in this category — a gym owner evaluating any serious platform expects all of them, and their absence reads as a hole:

| Feature | Prevalence | This product (per description given) |
|---|---|---|
| Automated recurring billing | Universal (every vendor surveyed) | **Has it** (renewals with expiry math described) |
| Failed-payment dunning / retry logic | Universal at mid-tier+ (explicit in Mindbody, WellnessLiving, ABC Ignite Revenue Optimization module) | **Not mentioned** — flag as a gap. No payment processor is in this product's scope at all (by design, "gyms bring their own processor"), so dunning may sit entirely outside this platform's boundary; worth an explicit product decision, not an assumption |
| Contract & waiver e-signature | Common (eFit, most access-control-integrated platforms, ABC Ignite, Mindbody) ([eFit Financial](https://efitfinancial.com/)) | **Not mentioned** — flag as a gap |
| Access control / door hardware integration (Kisi, Brivo, dormakaba) | Common at mid-tier+ (Gymflow, Perfect Gym, SportsNow, ABC Ignite, Gymtiva+Brivo) | **Not mentioned** — flag as a gap |
| Class waitlists | Universal in class-based platforms (Mindbody, Glofox, TeamUp, Momence, Xplor Triib all market this) | **Not mentioned** in the description — flag as a likely gap given the product explicitly does class reservations |
| Staff payroll / commission tracking | Common at mid-tier+ (Zen Planner, WellnessLiving, ABC Ignite) | **Not mentioned** — flag as a gap |
| Lead management / sales CRM pipeline | Common at mid-tier+ (Mindbody, Glofox, Zen Planner, ABC Ignite "Sales" module) | **Not mentioned** — flag as a gap |
| Automated marketing (email/SMS/WhatsApp campaigns) | Common (Clubworx explicitly ships automated SMS/email, ABC Ignite "Engagement" module, Hapana HQ tools include SMS) | **Not mentioned** — flag as a gap (emailed HTML receipts exist, but that's transactional, not marketing) |
| Retention / churn analytics | Common at mid-tier+ (Hapana "Dash," ABC Ignite "Insights," WellnessLiving reporting) | Monthly close/revenue reports exist, but churn/retention analytics specifically **not mentioned** — partial gap |
| Mobile staff app (not just admin web) | Common (Xplor Triib "connected desktop + kiosk + Android/iOS," PushPress, Wodify) | **Not mentioned** — the admin/front-desk app is described as a web app; unclear if it's mobile-responsive or has a native staff app — flag as ambiguous |
| Self-serve online signup & purchase | Universal (every vendor) | Ambiguous — self-activation via invite link is described, but not clear if a *prospect* (non-invited) can self-register and buy without staff involvement |
| Family / dependent accounts | Common (eFit, Kisi note "allow family members access... without additional registration work") | **Not mentioned** — flag as a gap |
| Appointments / 1-on-1 PT booking | Universal at mid-tier+ (Mindbody's origin use case, ABC Trainerize, Arketa, Momence) | **Not mentioned** — flag as a gap (product is class-session-centric, not appointment-centric) |
| Apple/Google Wallet member cards | Emerging but present (PassKit-style integrations, Kisi Apple Wallet passes) | **Not mentioned** — minor gap, low urgency |
| Reporting exports / accounting integrations | Common at mid-tier+ (most platforms cite QuickBooks/Xero-style exports) | Monthly close is described but export/accounting-integration is **not mentioned** — flag as a gap |
| Class-count packs / punch cards | Universal | **Has it** (class-count packs explicitly in scope) |
| Dated vs. unlimited memberships | Universal | **Has it** (explicitly in scope) |
| Front-desk POS w/ receipts | Universal | **Has it** (POS-style checkout, folio receipts, personalized sales) |
| Attendance check-in | Universal | **Has it** (class-session attendance check-in) |

**Summary of gaps:** the biggest, most-likely-to-be-noticed holes against the table stakes are **waitlists** (surprising given the product is reservation-centric), **e-signature waivers**, **lead/CRM pipeline**, **marketing automation**, **payroll/commission**, and **family accounts**. Several of these (waivers, payroll, CRM) may be deliberately out of scope for a lean MVP, but waitlists in particular sit close enough to the existing reservation feature that a gym owner comparing this to any incumbent will notice its absence immediately.

## Part 3 — Direct answers

### What does the branded/white-label member app cost as an add-on?
Concrete, cited numbers:
- **Mindbody**: $249–299/mo add-on, or bundled only at Ultimate Plus ($699+/mo) ([FitBudd](https://www.fitbudd.com/post/how-much-does-mindbody-cost)).
- **PushPress**: $97/mo add-on ([Wodify's PushPress pricing breakdown](https://www.wodify.com/blog/pushpress-pricing-plans)).
- **ABC Trainerize**: $169 **one-time** for a re-skinned app at the Pro tier; a true independent App-Store-listed white-label app is **included only** at Studio ($248–275/mo) ([trainerize.com/pricing](https://www.trainerize.com/pricing/)).
- **WellnessLiving**: white-label "Achieve" app gated to BusinessPro ($349/mo)+ ([schedulingkit.com WellnessLiving pricing guide](https://schedulingkit.com/pricing-guides/wellnessliving-pricing)).
- **Hapana**: white-labelled app reserved for the top modular bundle, from $635/mo ([hapana.com/pricing](https://www.hapana.com/pricing)).
- **ABC Glofox**: reported bundled at the Elite tier (~$250-400+/mo) but not itemized publicly ([Vibefam](https://vibefam.com/glofox-pricing-2026/)).
- **Virtuagym**: confirmed to exist as a genuine white-label app, price entirely sales-call-gated, not published ([business.virtuagym.com/custom-mobile-app](https://business.virtuagym.com/custom-mobile-app/)).
- **Exercise.com**: included in the base $239/mo+ plan — the one vendor surveyed that treats a branded app as the core product rather than an upsell ([exercise.com/platform/pricing](https://www.exercise.com/platform/pricing/)).

**Takeaway:** the market norm is to treat a branded member app as a premium upsell worth roughly **$100–350/month recurring** (or a several-hundred-dollar one-time fee at the cheap end), reserved for the top 1-2 pricing tiers. A platform that ships brand-per-tenant by default, at every tier, undercuts this specific monetization line directly — this is a stronger and more concrete wedge than the payment-processing argument for several vendors (Mindbody, WellnessLiving, Hapana, Glofox).

### What's the real revenue model — software fee or payment-processing take rate?
Both, but processing is frequently the larger and more hidden lever:
- **Mindbody**: SaaS subscription + 2.99-3.6%+$0.30 processing + 20% marketplace commission (≈23.5% combined on marketplace-sourced sales) ([Exercise.com "What percentage does Mindbody take?"](https://www.exercise.com/grow/what-percentage-does-mindbody-take/)).
- **PushPress**: an explicit tiered trade — Free tier = 4.99%+$0.30 take rate in lieu of subscription, down to 2.75%+$0.30 at the $229/mo tier ([pushpress.com/blog](https://www.pushpress.com/blog/what-does-gym-software-really-cost)).
- **Momence**: the purest example — Basic (free subscription) = 5% of every transaction; Pro ($60/mo) = 2.5%; Custom ($199/mo) = 0% — modeled out to ~$12.9k/yr vs ~$3.9k/yr vs ~$6.3k/yr total cost on $100k/yr studio revenue ([studiostackpro.com Momence pricing guide](https://studiostackpro.com/blog/momence-pricing-guide/)).
- **Arketa**: flat 3% platform fee stacked on top of standard card processing, regardless of tier ([Vibefam](https://vibefam.com/arketa-pricing-2026/)).
- **WellnessLiving**: mandatory in-house Paragon processor, rate undisclosed publicly but user-reported as excessive ("borderline predatory," [community.wellnessliving.com](https://community.wellnessliving.com/topic/paragon-ridiculous-fees/)).
- **ABC Fitness** (parent of Ignite/Glofox/Trainerize): processes $8.8B+/yr for 24,000+ clubs — payments processing is described by the company itself as core business, not ancillary ([ignite.abcfitness.com/revenue-optimization](https://ignite.abcfitness.com/revenue-optimization/)).
- **Counter-examples** (no forced take): Wodify (2.6-2.9% card / 1.5% ACH is presented as low, optional, not a profit center), Gymdesk, TeamUp, Gymflow, Zen Planner, Clubworx all price as flat SaaS with no confirmed forced processor rake.

**Quantified verdict**: this confirms "we take no cut" is a real, differentiated wedge specifically against the **legacy/enterprise tier** (Mindbody, WellnessLiving, ABC family, Arketa, Momence's free/pro tiers) where the take rate is 2.5-5% plus marketplace commissions up to 23.5% combined — but it is *already the norm*, not a novel wedge, against the **flat-rate SaaS challenger tier** this product most resembles in size (Gymdesk, TeamUp, Gymflow, Zen Planner). The wedge should be marketed as "we don't do what Mindbody/WellnessLiving/Momence/Arketa do," not as a category-wide first.

### Who is moving down-market, and at what price?
- **PushPress** ships an explicit **Free** tier funded by processing take rate, aimed squarely at the smallest CrossFit/boutique gyms, and is the most-recommended pick in Reddit gym-owner discussion for *new* gyms ([Vibefam Reddit synthesis](https://vibefam.com/gym-management-software-reddit-2026/)).
- **Gymdesk** (formerly Gym Insight) positions explicitly on transparent, member-count-tiered pricing from $75/mo, marketed directly against Mindbody-tier complexity/cost ([gymdesk.com/blog/gym-management-software-cost](https://gymdesk.com/blog/gym-management-software-cost)).
- **Gymflow** and **TeamUp** both market flat, no-lock-in pricing ($129/mo and $104-309/mo respectively) with explicit "no contracts" positioning aimed at owner-operators wary of Mindbody-style lock-in.
- **Momence** and **Zen Planner** are both cited in third-party "under $100/mo" and "$100-200/mo" buying guides as the default recommendation for small independent gyms ([Gymsense 2026 pricing guide](https://gymsense.io/blog/how-much-does-gym-management-software-cost-2026-pricing-guide)).
- Adjacent, smaller down-market entrants surfaced in this scan but **not in the original candidate list** — worth flagging to the requester: **1club** (from $63/mo, AI-native, explicitly targeting small/mid gyms) and **LegitFit** (targets independent studios/solo trainers) ([1club.ai blog](https://1club.ai/blog/best-gym-management-software-2026)). These would directly undercut a new entrant on price and are not yet profiled in depth here — worth a follow-up pass if this scan is extended.
