## What was ruled, in one paragraph

`E · VENTANA`. `CLIENTES` becomes **one flat list** — no sections, no fold, no tabs — with `estado` as a per-row column (`VIGENTE` / `VENCIDO` / `SIN CLASES`) and a ratio header (`N activos de M totales`, never a partition). `INICIO` gains **two bounded tiles** low on the page where `ÚLTIMAS ASISTENCIAS` sits: **POR RENOVAR** (live package **and** ≤10 días or ≤1 clase) and **AÚN A TIEMPO** (no live package, ended 1–15 días, two arms — expired by date vs classes ran out). Past day 16 a member leaves both tiles entirely and is reachable by search only. Absence is a quiet `{n}D SIN VENIR` badge on **paid-up members only** — never a tier, never a count, never a sort key.

## The one thing that will bite whoever executes this

**`POR RENOVAR` is now a single source of truth, and it does not currently mean what the ruling says.**

Today `clientes.tsx:52` derives it from `urgenciaCliente` returning `critico | urgente`. On RED that is **22 rows** — 13 already gone, 5 spent one-off passes, 4 genuinely renewable. The ruled predicate yields ~4. Shipping the tile without unifying puts the same words on two screens with two different numbers, which is structurally the same bug that started this map (`21 total · 9 vigentes · 13 por renovar` = 22 of 21).

So flooring `urgenciaCliente` (`packages/domain/src/rules.ts:149`) and reconciling its thresholds is a **prerequisite of the spec, not a follow-up**.

## Naming is doctrine, not decoration

Every label names **a package and a clock, never a person.** Fitco, CrossHero, Boxmagic, Xplor and Wodify all put a verdict on the human (`Cliente Inactivo`, `Slipping Away`, `Socio de Baja`, `Churned`, `Lost`). RED says the *paquete* is vigente or venció, that the gym is *aún a tiempo*, that the *lista* closes.

No vendor in the twenty-vendor scan holds that position. **Never reintroduce `Activo` / `Inactivo` applied to a person** — it collides with eight vendors *and* with `CONTEXT.md`, where `estado` is already a three-valued derived type whose values do not include `inactivo`.
