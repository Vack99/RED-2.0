# Niche blind-spot sweep — what the first two market docs left aside

**Date:** 2026-08-18

**Inputs:** `docs/brand/PRODUCT-BRIEF.md` §2 · `docs/Context/2026-08-08-membership-booking-market-taxonomy.md` · `docs/Context/2026-08-10-sales-niche-sweep.md`
**Method:** four independent opus agents, one per blind-spot lens (the individual rather than the venue · the buyer who isn't the venue · the shape divorced from sport and skills · adversarial re-litigation of the rejected traps). ~75 MX-targeted searches total, same fit rule and verdict scale as the source docs. This file records only what is **absent from** those two.

**The fit rule (unchanged):** a recurring paquete/plan with vigencia · sold against a scheduled session with a named roster · attendance taken.

## 3. The gap ranking is upside down

Source §7 leads with **GAP-A**. By market-unlocked ÷ cost-to-close that is last, not first.

| Rank | Gap | Minimal "closed" | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **GAP-C — guardian↔minor** | A nullable `responsable` relation on `cliente`, one auth account linked to N clientes in the same gym, a "familia" grouping. **Out of scope:** medical, allergy, pickup, photo consent | **The gap as written is not the blocker.** Every MX operator keeps carta responsiva, medical and pickup lists **on paper** and expects nothing from software. What actually breaks is narrower: **one payer, several enrolled children** — sibling discounts are standard and published (AQUALAND 10%, Barça Academy MX 10%). One migration, one roster change, one member-app screen — not a compliance build. Unlocks all of G8, kids' martial arts, kids' padel/tenis, camps, circo/parkour infantil |
| **2** | **GAP-B, light half only** | Optional `lugares` labels on a class type + seat pick at reserva | Two unlike things hide under one label. **Decline the hourly-court/peak-pricing half outright** — that's Playtomic's per-booking business. The light half is seat-numbering (reformer #4, bike #12), which protects rooms already scored STRONG |
| **3** | **GAP-E — B2B payer** | A `cliente` flagged empresa holding N member clientes; a `venta` whose payer is the empresa while sessions consume members' balances | Capped by CFDI. A MX B2B buyer expects a factura, and CFDI is do-not-claim — closing GAP-E without it closes half the door |
| **4** | **GAP-D — mostly a mislabel** | A paquete whose vigencia is a fixed date range + a `cupo cerrado` flag | Camps and block workshops need nothing built. **Decline the gradebook/level-progression half** — three products, not a feature |
| **5** | **GAP-A — biggest market, worst ratio** | — | Four builds (variable-duration per-practitioner slots, room/chair assignment, no-show deposits, NOM-004 expediente with 5-yr retention) into a saturated category, for buyers who don't need roster+attendance+expiry. The only cheap sliver is the capacity-1 standing weekly session already adjudicated in the sweep — and it needs no new feature |

## 4. Four new gaps named

- **GAP-F — no session at all.** Online coaching (asesoría en línea, $800–$1,600/mes) fits roster + plan + vigencia perfectly and makes **Agenda and Asistencia dead weight**. What that buyer sells is the programa. Don't chase.
- **GAP-G — no mostrador.** The product assumes money is collected at a desk (brief §6). The venue-less tier collects by SPEI/Mercado Pago between sessions, from a bus. Every MX tool that wins that tier leads with the payment rail (Feast Fit: "cobros recurrentes en pesos vía Stripe, desde México").
- **GAP-H — multi-sede tenancy.** One org, many locations: shared roster, per-location staff scope, consolidated reporting. `sede`/`sucursal`/`ubicación` appear **zero times** across `packages/`, `apps/` and `supabase/migrations/` — one tenant is one location, structurally. **Already table stakes in MX**: Connect Gym, Amyra, FITMA and XCORE all sell "multi-sucursal nativo". Don't enter chains without it.
- **GAP-I — third-party eligibility.** The attendee's right to be in the session comes from someone else's list (employer padrón, aggregator app, socio roll), not a `venta`. Breaks `esPrimeraCompra`/`vigencia` as the gate on attendance. Hits every institutional, amenity and gremio buyer.

## 5. Verdicts that stand — do not re-litigate a third time

Autoescuelas (no recurring class exists) · bootcamps/diplomados (the blocker is the term end date + SEP gradebook, **not** the billing shape — Anaderm publishes $1,275/mes) · catecismo (real money exists — one parish charges $150, another tiers $2,900/$1,500/$1,100 — but one cycle to a sacrament + per-child tiering + no SaaS budget) · consultorios/dentales/veterinarias/med-spas · ligas y torneos (team-as-payer destroys the fit rule; Sportwey owns it) · condominios (GAP-B + dense incumbents) · DIF/INAPAM ($25/taller is a token, not revenue) · maestros particulares a domicilio · colectivos informales ($300/año dues) · wine/coffee/bread subscription clubs (**the most seductive false positive** — the box ships, there is no roster to check in) · meal plans · board-game cafés · esports (per-station hourly; this *sources* the taxonomy's GAP-B guess) · supper clubs · motoclubes · lavandería · pensión de estacionamiento · transporte escolar (incumbents win on GPS, not rosters).

**Independent personal trainers: killed with numbers.** 39.3k ocupados, salario promedio **$7.47k MXN/mes**, 67.4% informales ([datamexico](https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/es/profile/occupation/entrenadores-deportivos-y-directores-tecnicos)). The median trainer earns less per month than a gym membership costs per year, and Feast Fit already prices the payable slice at $177 MXN/mes.

## 6. Open threads worth one pass each

1. **Colegios de profesionistas — the most likely wrong POOR in the set, unsourced.** CCPM runs "más de 300 cursos al año" under the Norma de Desarrollo Profesional Continuo, with member pricing and constancias — which contradicts "they administer dues, not attendance". Both pages are JS-rendered and returned no body. One manual browser pass at `contadoresmexico.org.mx` settles it. Would land on GAP-E + CFDI, not on scheduling.
2. **FlowPass pricing** — the closest MX-native competitor to iBookit's exact shape (alumnos, planes, asistencia, cobros, WhatsApp; priced by alumno count). Site is JS-rendered; `flow-pass.com/precios` returned empty. **The single most useful missing number in this research.**
3. **Universities as a paying segment** — UNAM confirms courses "para el público en general" but publishes no cuotas anywhere. Unresolved, not POOR.
4. **An outsourced extraescolares vendor category in MX** — searches returned Spanish companies and MX schools running their own. The MX route into schools looks like franchise-brand-into-school (Little Kickers, RobotiX), not a services vendor. Unverified negative.

## 7. Two structural findings for GTM

- **No Mexican multi-sede martial-arts franchise surfaced at all.** Searches returned Spanish (Mugendo, MAC) and US brands. **G1 — the #1 ranked target — is structurally independent single-dojo operators in MX.** Good for the current ICP, bad for any franchise motion in the strongest genre.
- **Institutions do charge, at private-market prices.** COMUDE León: natación $409/mes + $199 insc.; COMUDE Zapopan $531–$798/mes with reinscripción on late payment. The product shape passes. **CFDI + procurement is the gate**, not features — which makes the outstanding SAT details load-bearing for the whole institutional column.
