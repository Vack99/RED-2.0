# A — "El lugar" · construction

- **Grid:** 64u square, 3x3 on a 20u pitch (12u cell, 8u gutter, 2.5u stroke → 14.5u outer footprint, 4.75u optical margin). Every file reuses this geometry unredrawn; the app icon is only `translate(64 64) scale(6)`.
- **Logic:** open cells are 2.5u-stroke squircles (rx 3.5) = the slots still free; the claimed spot is the same cell, solid, at column 3 / row 2 — off centre horizontally, but sitting on the horizontal mirror axis so the mark stays calm instead of tipping.
- **Optical correction:** the solid cell is drawn 13u against the open cells' 14.5u outer footprint (~10% inside). First render had it at 15u and the plum visibly bulged — filled mass reads considerably larger than an outline of equal size. Verified by headless render at 160/64/32/24/16px, not by arithmetic.
- **Ownable tic:** the claimed cell's top-left corner is square while its other three are rx 3.5 — the booked spot is pinned back into the grid it came from, and it picks up a faint bookmark read the name earns. Reads from ~24px up; below that it just softens.
- **Wordmark:** Hanken Grotesk 600 at 43u, -0.02em tracking with +0.9u put back between `i` and `B` so the cap-B seam holds at any size; baseline 47 sits ~1u above the mark's centre to offset x-height-heavy mass. Accent tittle tried and dropped — the idea is *exactly one* filled spot, and a second plum spends it.
- **App icon:** plum tile beat the paper alternative — the operator's home screen is the one surface a recessive brand may own a colour slot on, and open-vs-filled carries the concept without needing the accent hue. Paper on plum 6.0:1; plum on light canvas 6.0:1; light accent on dark canvas 6.5:1 — all clear of the 3:1 decorative floor.
- **Weaknesses:** below ~20px the ring holes close and the bare mark reads as nine soft dots — ship the app icon at those sizes. Open cells are strokes, so flatten before any icon-font conversion. And the honest one: a 3x3 rounded-square grid lives next door to every "apps / menu" glyph ever drawn, so the off-axis fill and the square corner are carrying the whole differentiation load.
