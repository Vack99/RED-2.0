# Concept B — "Apartado"

- **Grid:** 96×96 artboard, cap 72 (y12–84), **stem 12 = the module**; every other stroke derives from it — bars and bowls 11 (stem−1, the standard optical correction for horizontals reading heavy), waist band 11. B width 44 (0.61×cap), deliberately narrow: that narrowness is what buys the counter a portrait proportion.
- **The bookmark is the lower counter, not an added element.** Counter x39–60 / y47–73 (21×26), rounded top r4, and its bottom edge replaced by a swallowtail — an ink triangle rises from the bottom bar to apex (49.5, 62), 11 deep = 42% of the counter. Both counters are left-aligned on x39 so they read as one interrupted column.
- **Merged counters (the brief's second option) were tried and rejected:** merging forces either a ~3:1 stroke collapse at the waist or a smooth right side that reads D. The waist joint + two counters is what keeps it unmistakably B at 16px, so the ribbon lives in the lower counter alone.
- **Optical corrections over math:** waist joint at y37, above centre, so the lower bowl and its ribbon get the room; mark sits +1 right of geometric centre against the heavy stem; upper-bowl stroke tapers into the waist joint (11→9.4) rather than bulging; notch apex blunted r2.5 and tail tips r1 so nothing renders as a needle.
- **Lockup = mark beside the full name.** Replacing the wordmark's B was rejected: the mark already carries a bookmark inside a B, so a second one inside the word doubles the device, and the sacred i/B seam is held better by a real capital B. Mark cap 42 vs wordmark cap 34.4 (1.22×), baseline-aligned, optical gap ~22.
- **App icon = accent tile + light knockout,** not the inverse: a light tile vanishes on a home screen, and knocking out turns the ribbon into tile-colour, the strongest read of the negative space available. Contrast 5.5:1 light / 7.6:1 dark / 5.5:1 knockout — all clear of the 3:1 decorative floor.
- **Why ownable:** the bookmark is *inside* the letter and can't be lifted off it; it says *apartar tu lugar* in one glyph without any vertical's equipment, so it passes the three-businesses test (piano school / climbing gym / dance academy) unchanged.
- **Known weaknesses:** below ~28px the swallowtail flattens to a dark notch and the mark degrades to a plain B — graceful, but the concept is gone; the 0.61-width B reads slightly condensed beside a wide wordmark; the wordmark is live `<text>` sized against the Arial fallback, so re-measure the viewBox and convert to outlines once Hanken Grotesk is licensed.
