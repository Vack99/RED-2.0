# Concept C — La ficha

**Grid.** 256 master, 2-unit grid. Card x24–232 × y46–230 (208×184, ratio 1.13), corner r18; uniform 16 stroke drawn as one filled evenodd contour, aperture x40–216 × y62–214 at r2 (= 18−16, concentric). Tab x58–126 × y28–46 (68×18) with top corners r7 and concave base fillets r6 — one continuous outer contour, nothing glued on. The aperture stops at the top edge, so the tab is solid ink for free: the writing field is the hole, the label is the ink.
**Why this tab and no other.** Three earlier drafts died on the render: a narrow inset hump read as a *camera*, a flush-left 43% step read as the *OS folder*, a near-centred tab read as a *clipboard clip*. It only reads as an index tab at all three constraints at once — inset 16u clear of the corner arc (folders are flush-left), 3.8:1 wide-and-shallow (camera humps are narrow), and centre 17.3% of the card width left of axis (clips sit centred).
**Ownable.** Tab and clay bar share one axis *and* one width (x58–126): the label outside the card and the name inside it are the same 68u field, stacked. That rhyme, not the rounded rectangle, is the mark.
**Optical.** Tab corners r7 = 39% of tab height vs card r18 = 10% of card height — small elements need proportionally larger radii to read as equally round. Vertical placement is solved, not assumed: the solid tab's mass cancels the hollow body's low centroid at card-top = 46, putting the area centroid at y 128.3. The residual 3.5u horizontal centroid pull (1.4% of canvas) is left uncorrected — correcting it would visibly crowd the right margin.
**Accent.** One element. Pill 68×16 (height = stroke) at the aperture's upper third, top gap 40 = 2.5× stroke, left gap 18 vs right gap 90 so it reads as a written line, never a centred slot. 5.15:1 on #FAF8F4 and 7.3:1 on #16130E — decorative-safe, earthy, no alarm-red read.
**Lockup.** Card body = 1.36× cap height, sitting on the baseline; the tab occupies the ascender zone. Mark scaled 0.42 so its 6.7 stroke sits against the 600-weight's ~9.2 stems — deliberately a touch lighter, since a closed contour reads heavier than its stroke.
**App icon.** Clay tile beat paper tile: at 32px a near-white tile with a thin outline washes out in a home-screen grid. Card solidified to a knockout and the bar knocked back to clay — two colours, ~31% paper mass, no interior detail left to lose.
**Weaknesses.** (1) It lives or dies on the tab geometry above; a careless redraw falls straight back into folder or clipboard. (2) Lockups use live `<text>` — the Arial fallback shifts metrics, so bounds and the 28u gap are true only with Hanken Grotesk loaded; outline it before print or third-party handoff. (3) The clay bar is first to die below ~20px — the tab alone carries it there, as briefed. (4) Aperture r2 is near-sharp inside an r18 outer, forced by stroke 16 vs r18; visible only above ~120px.
